The 9-Step GigCredit Flywheel

How our closed-loop escrow engine transforms fragmented gig data into low-risk institutional capital.

01

User Conversion & Portable ID

Worker unlocks a personalized, portable financial identity backed by cross-platform work credentials.

02

Consent-Based Data Ingestion

Account Aggregator (AA) banking rails & SMS parsing extract live payout notifications across Swiggy, Uber & Zepto.

03

Behavioral Underwriting Engine

Calculates Multi-App Income Velocity, Stability Index, and Operational Trust (completion rate & ratings).

04

Programmatic API Marketplace

Anonymized risk profiles are broadcasted to institutional lending partners via automated real-time APIs.

05

Reverse-Auction Compete Bidding

NBFCs and Digital Banks programmatically bid to offer lower interest rates in under 60 seconds.

06

Digital Wallet Escrow Provisioning

Approved loan capital is disbursed into a secure GigCredit-linked Virtual Escrow Account.

07

Payout Routing Direction

Worker sets default work app payout destination to their GigCredit Virtual Escrow Account.

08

Closed-Loop Repayment Gateway

Micro-EMIs (e.g. $5/day) are extracted at source; remaining earnings are instantly swept to savings.

09

Institutional Data Flywheel

Near-zero default rates lower cost of capital, unlocking cheaper interest rates for all workers.
